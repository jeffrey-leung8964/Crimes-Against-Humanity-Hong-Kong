# Report on Crimes Against Humanity Committed by Hong Kong Government Officials in Hong Kong and Several Other Places

[Webpage](https://jeffrey-leung8964.github.io/Crimes-Against-Humanity-Hong-Kong)

After World War II, the [Judges’ Trial](https://nuremberg.law.harvard.edu/transcripts/3-transcript-for-nmt-3-justice-case) at Nuremberg (United States of America v. Josef Altstötter et al.) examined how legal institutions could be used to commit international crimes. More recently, [U.S. Senate Resolution 444](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/senate-resolution/444) condemned Xi Jinping for orchestrating crimes against humanity and highlighted the repression of fundamental freedoms in Hong Kong. These examples raise a central question: when can officials be held personally criminally responsible for repression carried out through state institutions?

The author began this independent research in June 2025, before enrolling at the University of California, Berkeley (UC Berkeley). In July 2025 the author launched a public [petition](https://www.change.org/p/launch-icc-investigation-over-crimes-against-humanity-committed-by-hong-kong-officials) calling on the Office of the Prosecutor of the International Criminal Court to act, and the report has been updated continuously since then as the situation in Hong Kong has developed. After enrolling, the author joined the **Human Rights Center Investigations Lab at UC Berkeley School of Law** as a **student researcher**. This report remains an independent project based entirely on publicly available materials. The web edition in this GitHub repository was first formally released on 30 October 2026.

Last update: 6 November 2026

The [Berkeley Protocol on Digital Open Source Investigations](https://www.ohchr.org/en/publications/policy-and-methodological-publications/berkeley-protocol-digital-open-source) offers guidance on collecting, verifying, and analyzing open-source information. Human rights defenders can also use it when documenting abuses and seeking accountability in their own contexts.

This report argues that, since 2019, the Hong Kong government's conduct has constituted a widespread or systematic attack against a civilian population, amounting to crimes against humanity under international criminal law.

It examines political imprisonment, persecution, torture, and transnational repression. It also considers whether officials in the executive, police, prosecution, judiciary, and correctional services could bear individual criminal responsibility for documented conduct.

The report focuses on the jurisdiction of the International Criminal Court (ICC). China is not a party to the Rome Statute, but the report examines whether territorial or nationality links to a State Party could allow the ICC to exercise jurisdiction over individual Hong Kong officials.

National courts may also pursue accountability through universal jurisdiction. Torture is also covered by the jurisdictional rules in Articles 5(2) and 7 of the UN Convention against Torture (CAT). When an alleged offender is present in territory under a State Party's jurisdiction, that State must establish jurisdiction and, if it does not extradite the person, submit the case to its competent authorities for consideration of prosecution.

Beyond individual criminal responsibility, the [Responsibility to Protect (R2P)](https://www.globalr2p.org/what-is-r2p/), unanimously adopted by UN Member States at the 2005 World Summit, affirms that every State must protect its population from genocide, war crimes, ethnic cleansing and crimes against humanity, and that the international community has a responsibility to help protect populations through diplomatic, humanitarian and other peaceful means. In May 2021 the General Assembly adopted [resolution 75/277](https://docs.un.org/en/a/res/75/277), placing R2P and the prevention of these crimes on its annual agenda with a yearly report from the Secretary-General.

The veto that China holds as a permanent member of the Security Council does not reach the United Nations' other mechanisms, as Liechtenstein, a State of some 40,000 people, has twice shown. In 2016, after Russia and China, both permanent members, had [jointly vetoed](https://media.un.org/photo/en/asset/oun7/oun7264953) the Council's referral of Syria to the ICC in May 2014, Liechtenstein [led the General Assembly to establish](https://www.llv.li/en/national-administration/diplomatic-representations/new-york-usa-/priorities-at-the-united-nations) the [International, Impartial and Independent Mechanism (IIIM)](https://iiim.un.org/), which collects and preserves evidence of the most serious crimes committed in Syria for use in national and international courts. In 2022 its ["veto initiative"](https://docs.un.org/en/a/res/76/262) was adopted by consensus: every veto cast by a permanent member now [automatically convenes a General Assembly debate](https://lisd.princeton.edu/announcements/2026/liechtenstein-institute-self-determination-hosts-4th-annual-un-veto-initiative) within ten working days, at which the member that cast the veto is invited to explain itself first. For the people of Hong Kong the implication is direct: by the commitment of the UN membership itself, the crimes against humanity documented in this report are not an internal affair of China but a matter the international community has undertaken to prevent and respond to; the General Assembly has a standing item under which the situation in Hong Kong can be raised, reported on and acted upon every year; and an evidence mechanism for Hong Kong on the Syrian model could be created by the Assembly without a single vote in the Security Council.

This repository contains a factual summary, an analysis of individual responsibility, supporting evidence, and translations. These materials are available for review, further investigation, and accountability efforts.

# 關於香港政府官員在香港及其他若干地方犯下反人類罪的報告

[網頁版](https://jeffrey-leung8964.github.io/Crimes-Against-Humanity-Hong-Kong)

第二次世界大戰後，紐倫堡的「[法官審判（Judges’ Trial）](https://nuremberg.law.harvard.edu/transcripts/3-transcript-for-nmt-3-justice-case)」（United States of America v. Josef Altstötter et al.）探討了法律機構如何被用來犯下國際罪行。近年，美國參議院[第444號決議](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/senate-resolution/444)譴責習近平策劃反人類罪，並指出香港基本自由受到壓制。這些例子帶出本研究的核心問題：當政府透過制度進行鎮壓時，官員在甚麼情況下須承擔個人刑事責任？

作者於2025年6月展開這項獨立研究，其後入讀加州大學柏克萊分校（UC Berkeley）。2025年7月，作者公開發起[聯署](https://www.change.org/p/launch-icc-investigation-over-crimes-against-humanity-committed-by-hong-kong-officials)，要求國際刑事法院檢察官辦公室採取行動；本報告亦因應香港局勢的變化持續更新至今。入學後，作者加入加州大學柏克萊分校法學院（UC Berkeley School of Law）的人權中心調查實驗室（Human Rights Center Investigations Lab），擔任學生研究員（student researcher）。本報告仍屬獨立研究，全部資料均來自公開來源。本 GitHub 儲存庫的網頁版於2026年10月30日首次正式發佈。

最後更新：2026年11月6日

《[伯克利數字開源調查規程](https://www.ohchr.org/en/publications/policy-and-methodological-publications/berkeley-protocol-digital-open-source)》介紹蒐集、核實及分析開源資料的方法。世界各地的人權工作者也可參考這份規程，記錄侵害行為並推動問責。

本報告主張，香港政府自2019年以來的行為構成針對平民人口的廣泛或有系統的攻擊，達到國際刑法下反人類罪的構成標準。

報告分析政治監禁、迫害、酷刑及跨國鎮壓，並探討行政、警務、檢控、司法及懲教部門的官員，是否須為有資料記錄的行為承擔個人刑事責任。

報告主要討論國際刑事法院（ICC）的管轄權。雖然中國不是《羅馬規約》締約國，但報告探討有關案件是否可能因為與締約國存在屬地或國籍上的連繫，使國際刑事法院仍可對個別香港官員行使管轄權。

各國法院也可能透過普遍管轄權追究責任。酷刑另受聯合國《禁止酷刑公約》（CAT）第5(2)及第7條的管轄規則規範。當涉嫌犯罪者身處締約國管轄的領土內，該國須確立管轄權；如果不引渡此人，便須將案件交由主管機關考慮起訴。

在個人刑事責任之外，2005年世界首腦會議上獲聯合國會員國全體通過的「[保護責任](https://www.globalr2p.org/what-is-r2p/)」（Responsibility to Protect，R2P）原則訂明：每個國家都有責任保護其人民免遭種族滅絕、戰爭罪、種族清洗及反人類罪；國際社會亦有責任透過外交、人道及其他和平手段協助保護人民。2021年5月，聯合國大會通過[第75/277號決議](https://docs.un.org/en/a/res/75/277)，把保護責任及預防上述罪行列為每年例行議程項目，並要求秘書長每年提交報告。

中國作為安全理事會常任理事國所擁有的否決權，管不到聯合國的其他機制——人口約四萬的小國列支敦士登已兩度證明這一點。2014年5月，同為常任理事國的俄羅斯與中國[聯手否決](https://media.un.org/photo/en/asset/oun7/oun7264953)安理會把敘利亞局勢移交國際刑事法院的決議草案；2016年，列支敦士登[推動聯合國大會設立](https://www.llv.li/en/national-administration/diplomatic-representations/new-york-usa-/priorities-at-the-united-nations)「[國際公正獨立機制](https://iiim.un.org/)」（IIIM），蒐集和保存敘利亞境內最嚴重罪行的證據，供各國法院及國際法庭起訴之用。2022年，列支敦士登的「[否決權倡議](https://docs.un.org/en/a/res/76/262)」以協商一致方式獲通過：此後常任理事國每在安理會投下一張否決票，大會都會在十個工作天內[自動召開辯論](https://lisd.princeton.edu/announcements/2026/liechtenstein-institute-self-determination-hosts-4th-annual-un-veto-initiative)，投否決票的國家須首先解釋。對香港人而言，含意很直接：按照聯合國會員國自身作出的承諾，本報告記錄的反人類罪並非中國的內政，而是國際社會已承諾預防和回應的事項；聯合國大會設有常設議程項目，香港的情況可以在其下每年被提出、記錄和跟進；而仿照敘利亞模式為香港設立證據機制，大會毋須安理會任何一票便可做到。

本儲存庫收錄事實摘要、個人責任分析、證據資料及翻譯文件，供讀者查閱、進一步調查及推動問責。
